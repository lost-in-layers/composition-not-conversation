CATEGORY: attribute_binding

These questions request an attribute of a target selected by another attribute or relation. Include the attribute-bearing target layer plus every distinct layer required to bind the referring expression correctly.

If the target layer itself visibly contains both the requested attribute and the selecting property, it may be sufficient alone. If a landmark or owner is needed to distinguish the target, include it as reference_landmark or reference_entity. A selecting layer should usually precede the answer-bearing layer through reference_resolution or attribute_binding.

Do not add a landmark when the target is already uniquely and explicitly represented by one retained layer.
