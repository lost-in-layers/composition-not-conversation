CATEGORY: multi_hop_reference

Multi-hop questions identify the answer through a chain of at least two references or relations. Shard 1 states the answer type. Subsequent shards expose the chain one link at a time while naming target and intermediate entities clearly. Preserve chain direction and do not collapse away an intermediate reference.

Examples:

Canonical: What object is to the right of the object underneath the cat?
Atomic units: "What" (intent); "object" (answer type); "to the right of the object" (target relation); "underneath the cat" (intermediate reference relation)
Shards:
1. Identify a particular thing in the image.
2. The answer should name an object.
3. The target object is to the right of an intermediate object.
4. The intermediate object is underneath the cat.

Canonical: What fruit is being held by the woman who is positioned to the left of the young child?
Atomic units: "What" (intent); "fruit" (answer type); "being held by the woman" (target relation/reference); "positioned to the left of the young child" (intermediate relation)
Shards:
1. Identify a particular thing in the image.
2. The answer should name a fruit.
3. The target fruit is being held by a woman.
4. The referenced woman is positioned to the left of the young child.

Canonical: Which street fixture is positioned to the right of the bench that appears below and to the left of the man standing in the upper-right part of the scene?
Atomic units: "Which" (intent); "street fixture" (answer type); "positioned to the right of the bench" (target relation/reference); "below" (bench relation); "to the left of the man" (bench relation/reference); "standing in the upper-right part of the scene" (man condition)
Shards:
1. Identify a particular thing in the scene.
2. The answer should name a street fixture.
3. The target street fixture is positioned to the right of a bench.
4. The referenced bench appears below and to the left of a man.
5. The referenced man is standing in the upper-right part of the scene.
