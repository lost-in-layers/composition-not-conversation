CATEGORY: direct_ask

Direct questions usually contain a requested answer type/property and a target. Keep a bare target separate from the intent. If the question includes genuine evidence conditions, separate those conditions without inventing a richer referring expression.

Examples:

Canonical: What is the color of the bulb?
Atomic units: "What" (intent); "color" (answer type); "the bulb" (target)
Shards:
1. Determine a property of a particular object in the image.
2. The requested property is its color.
3. The target object is the bulb.

Canonical: What objects are hanging on the clothesline?
Atomic units: "What" (intent); "objects" (answer type); "hanging on the clothesline" (target condition)
Shards:
1. Identify particular things in the image.
2. The answer should name objects.
3. The target objects are hanging on the clothesline.

Canonical: What sport is the athlete in the image most likely playing, given that he is standing on a marked court and holding a racket in a serving posture?
Atomic units: "What" (intent); "sport" (answer type); "the athlete in the image" (target); "standing on a marked court" (condition); "holding a racket in a serving posture" (condition)
Shards:
1. Identify what activity a particular person is most likely performing.
2. The answer should name a sport.
3. The target person is the athlete in the image.
4. The athlete is standing on a marked court.
5. The athlete is holding a racket in a serving posture.
