CATEGORY: attribute_binding

Attribute-binding questions ask for a property of one entity selected by attributes or relations. Shard 1 asks for the property. Later shards establish the target entity and each answer-selecting modifier. Do not detach a modifier so far that it could bind to the wrong entity.

Examples:

Canonical: what is the color of the cat that is looking up?
Atomic units: "what" (intent); "color" (answer type); "the cat" (target); "looking up" (attribute)
Shards:
1. Determine a property of a particular animal in the image.
2. The requested property is its color.
3. The target animal is the cat.
4. The target cat is looking up.

Canonical: What color is the object on the desk to the right of the cat?
Atomic units: "What" (intent); "color" (answer type); "the object on the desk" (target); "to the right of the cat" (relation)
Shards:
1. Determine a property of a particular object.
2. The requested property is its color.
3. The target object is on the desk.
4. The target object is to the right of the cat.

Canonical: What color is the long neck of the bird positioned behind and slightly above the other bird?
Atomic units: "What" (intent); "color" (answer type); "the long neck of the bird" (target); "positioned behind" (relation); "slightly above the other bird" (relation/reference)
Shards:
1. Determine a property of a particular part of a bird.
2. The requested property is its color.
3. The target part is the bird's long neck.
4. The target bird is positioned behind the other bird.
5. The target bird is slightly above the other bird.
