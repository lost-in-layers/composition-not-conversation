CATEGORY: counting

Counting questions require a count and may also request locations or impose inclusion conditions. Shard 1 must retain every requested output operation (for example, count and report locations). Later shards define the population and scope. Never introduce a number.

Examples:

Canonical: How many computer mice are visible on the desk?
Atomic units: "How" (intent); "many" (answer type); "computer mice" (target); "visible on the desk" (scope)
Shards:
1. Determine a quantity for particular things in the image.
2. The requested quantity is a count.
3. The things to count are computer mice.
4. Include the computer mice visible on the desk.

Canonical: How many men present in the image?
Atomic units: "How" (intent); "many" (answer type); "men" (target); "present in the image" (scope)
Shards:
1. Determine a quantity for a particular kind of person.
2. The requested quantity is a count.
3. The people to count are men present in the image.

Canonical: How many living things were there in the image and where are they in the image?
Atomic units: "How" (intent); "many" (answer type); "living things" (target); "in the image" (scope); "where are they in the image" (second requested output)
Shards:
1. Determine information about a set of things in the image.
2. One requested result is their count.
3. The things to count are the living things.
4. Include the living things present in the image.
5. Also report where those living things are in the image.
